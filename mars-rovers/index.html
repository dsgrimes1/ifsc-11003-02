Robotic Explorers of Mars

How do we explore a planet millions of miles away? Meet the rovers helping scientists investigate Mars.

Meet the Rovers

Mars rovers are robotic vehicles that explore the surface of Mars. They carry cameras and scientific instruments to study rocks, investigate the environment, and help us understand the planet.

Curiosity

Curiosity investigates whether Mars once had conditions that could support microbial life. Its instruments allow scientists to examine rocks and soil in Gale Crater.

Explore the Curiosity mission

Perseverance

Perseverance explores Jezero Crater, searching for signs of ancient microbial life and collecting rock samples for potential return to Earth.
